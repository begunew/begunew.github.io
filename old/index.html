About the center

A place to work through what matters.

The Family Therapy Center is a training center for master's and doctoral MFT interns from Lee University. Graduate interns provide therapy under the supervision of licensed clinicians and graduate faculty.

We work with individuals, couples, and families on a wide range of concerns.
